(I promise I know ball) but I really enjoy the inner turmoil thats represented in Anakins character, many may say that he is onoxious and whiny and yes that can be true but understanding his behavior and where it comes from makes him a well written character. Plo Koon

Plo Koon (or papa plo as I like to call him) is a extremely wise and compassionate Jedi master, leading a group of clone troopers called the 'wolf pack' or the 104th battalion with deep care. I honestly just really admire the way he speaks and treats the clones and Ahsoka; very fatherly.

Clone Troopers

Where do I even start? I will always remember the nights that were spent sitting down in front of my TV and watching the clone wars series and how 9/10 times it left me crying. Clone troopers; the army of the republic born on Kamino. Those of wich are replicated copies of the well renowned bounty hunter Jango fett.

Brothers in arms, are brothers for life. No matter what piece of media you consume that includes clones, the devotion they show to each other and their job; or even if a clone has gone AWOL (called desertion) there is usually still a deep bond between them or a forming one. Seeing the private life of a numerous amount of clone troopers really shows that even if they are identical since creation, they are all so extremely different. Makes me cry so much omg.
